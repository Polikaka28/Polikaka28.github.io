Privacy Policy

Evdanco Tender Agent · Last updated 15 September 2026

Evdanco Tender Agent ("the app") is an internal automation operated by Evdanco Group. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is used.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by Evdanco Group staff. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside Evdanco signs in to it.

Google data the app accesses

The app connects to the Gmail account of the Evdanco user who authorised it. It uses that access only to send the daily tender report email, with its attachment, to Evdanco recipients.

The app does not read, store, analyse or delete any existing emails, contacts or other Google account data.

How data is stored

The Google authorisation token is stored encrypted inside Evdanco's own n8n automation instance. Tender data comes from public procurement notices and is kept in that same instance.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Report content is sent only to Evdanco recipients.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the Gmail credential in n8n.
