Evdanco Tender Agent

Internal tender-monitoring tool of Evdanco Group

Evdanco Tender Agent is an internal automation used by Evdanco Group. Once a day it reads publicly available procurement notices (such as NIS a.d. open tenders and the EU TED notice portal), screens them for relevance to Evdanco's oil & gas drilling and workover business, and emails a short report to the Evdanco team.

The tool is not offered to the public. It connects to Google only to send that report from Evdanco's own Gmail account.
